# Room 1: Junior Security Analyst

## What is a Security Operations Center (SOC)?

A Security Operations Center (SOC) is a team responsible for continuously monitoring, detecting, investigating, and responding to cybersecurity threats within an organization.

The SOC works 24/7 to protect systems, networks, applications, and users from cyber attacks.

---

## Who is a Junior SOC Analyst?

A Junior SOC Analyst is the first line of defense in a Security Operations Center. They monitor security alerts, investigate suspicious activities, and escalate incidents when necessary.

Although this is an entry-level role, it requires strong analytical thinking and attention to detail.

---

## Daily Responsibilities of a Junior SOC Analyst

- Monitor security alerts from SIEM platforms
- Investigate suspicious events
- Analyze logs from Windows, Linux, firewalls, and endpoints
- Identify indicators of compromise (IOCs)
- Escalate confirmed incidents to senior analysts
- Document investigation findings
- Follow incident response procedures
- Continuously monitor the organization's security posture

# Room 2: SOC and Blue Team

## What is the Blue Team?

The Blue Team is responsible for defending an organization's infrastructure against cyber attacks.

Their primary objective is to prevent, detect, and respond to security incidents.

---

## Relationship Between the SOC and Blue Team

The SOC is a key component of the Blue Team.

While the Blue Team focuses on the overall defense strategy, the SOC performs the day-to-day monitoring, detection, investigation, and response activities.

In simple words:

**Blue Team = Defensive Security Team**

**SOC = Operational team that performs defensive monitoring and incident response.**

---

## Blue Team Responsibilities

- Monitor security events
- Detect malicious activity
- Investigate alerts
- Perform incident response
- Threat hunting
- Malware analysis
- Vulnerability management
- Improve security posture
- Create security reports
